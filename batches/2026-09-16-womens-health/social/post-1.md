## Slides

1. **Cover** (family photo, cream wall): `How women have been healing across the world.` / `Four remedies you can find in your kitchen`
2. **Context** (faded ginger photo): `A lot of women's medicine never lived in a pharmacy. It lived in the kitchen.` / `Seeds in a jar. A root by the stove. A pot of something warm. Four traditions, four remedies you may already own.`
3. **01 · Naturopathy · For nausea in early pregnancy · Fresh ginger tea.** Grate a thumb of ginger into hot water. A 2014 review of 1,278 people found it eased early pregnancy nausea as well as vitamin B6. *Up to four cups a day. Check with your midwife first.*
4. **02 · Ayurveda · For irregular cycles with PCOS · Fennel seed decoction.** Boil the seeds, let it cool, sip it daily. In a small 2018 trial of 30 women, it kept pace with the pill on cycle regularity. *Never swap prescribed care without your doctor.*
5. **03 · Unani · For a bloated belly · Ajwain seed water.** A pinch of carom seeds stirred into warm water, sipped before you eat. Unani uses it to warm digestion and settle gas. *Sold as ajwain or carom seed in most spice aisles.*
6. **04 · African (the Cape) · For the menopause years · Honeybush tea.** Two or three unsweetened cups a day. A Cape herbal remedy for menopausal symptoms, rich in the same antioxidants as rooibos. *Brew it from dried honeybush leaves.*
7. **End hook · Save:** `Save this for your next shop.` / `Four jars worth keeping. Find the rest on remedae.app` + the four remedies as a quick-glance list + the educational disclaimer.

Every fact on slides 3 to 6 is taken from the live remedy cards (ids: nausea-for-pregnancy-naturopathy-1, pcos-ayurveda-2, bloating-unani-1, perimenopause-african-1).
