Ginger tea in the first trimester. A warm compress on the first day of a period. Sage for the two a.m. heat. Most of it was never prescribed. It was passed down.

We built a women's health shelf: 23 conditions live, every tradition read side by side with modern medicine, safety notes on every card. And we wrote about what building it showed us: the remedies women actually use sit mostly outside the research record. Not disproven. Unproven. There is a difference.

The full piece is on the journal, and the shelf is at remedae.app/womens-health. Traditionally used, not medical advice.
