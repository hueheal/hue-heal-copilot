# Remedae: revised strategy and plan, September 2026

Compiled by the Chief of staff from six department reviews of the July 2026 documents (business plan v3, investor pack and market validation, financial plan, investor deck 4) against the facts of 8 September 2026. Department files sit in `review/strategy-2026-09/`. Every number is marked known, assumed or missing. Nothing here is medical advice, and where a solicitor or accountant is named, one is needed.

## The verdict in five lines

1. The shape of the July plan holds: a free library first, Remedae+ only once readers return, research and commerce after that. No department wants a different company.
2. The timing and the claims do not hold. No round has closed, the product is live but gated at remedae.app with 105 published conditions, and the deck, plan and model still say 240+, complete, medically reviewed and remedae.com.
3. Every downstream number inherits an assumption that has not been observed: 5,000 readers at launch. Until readership and return are measured, the model is a story, not a forecast.
4. One blocker sits under Growth, Partnerships, Experts and Counsel alike: a named clinical advisor. Without one, "medically reviewed" cannot be said, creators have nobody to ask who checks the shelf, and the deck cannot circulate.
5. The plan for the next 90 days is therefore narrow: correct the claims this week, measure return on the 105, name an advisor, and rebuild the model from the real starting position so the round opens on facts in early 2027.

## What still holds

- A library, not a clinic. Free to read, advisory language on every page, no diagnosis, no personalisation. Counsel confirms this is what keeps Remedae outside medical device rules.
- Thirteen traditions side by side with an evidence grade, modern medicine alongside and never beneath. Published pages average six traditions (known).
- The honesty position is the moat: contested traditions labelled as contested, "skip beats fabricate", every passage traced to a named body and a verbatim quote.
- UK first, diaspora first. The smallest viable audience is a UK adult raised with home remedies, now caring for a child or a parent, searching late at night.
- The partner order: advisor, then creators, then research, then API. Each opens the next.
- The pre-seed shape: one round funding an 18-month run to retention proof, seed raised on that proof.
- The build sequence in `docs/action-sequence.md`. Sprints 1 and 2 proved its ordering rule: unblock before widen, data before surface.

## Revised strategy

One outcome for the quarter: a reader arriving with a condition finds a page, opens more than one tradition, and comes back within 30 days. Everything below serves that or clears the way to the round.

1. Tell the truth in every document this week. Apply Counsel's ten rewrites (below). The shelf is 105 published and 240 mapped, source-verified by an automated pipeline, clinical review beginning on a named date.
2. Measure before widening. Instrument 7 and 30 day return, waitlist size and search misses. No new conditions beyond the 4 drafts, no light theme, no desktop compare, no recipes or shopping list this quarter.
3. Make search never dead-end and make the page show the comparison. Finish the queue and worker, pull typeahead forward, ship the honest research page, then the ingredient layer for the top 60 ingredients and the flattened card. This is the sequence's Sprints 2 and 3, unchanged.
4. Open the door in waves. Replace the hard gate with dated invitation waves, and test opening the ten most-searched conditions to anonymous readers with a waitlist prompt at the end. Ungate fully at 105 once misses are under 10% and the red-flag interstitial is live.
5. Name the clinical advisor and start a paid safety review. One pharmacist and one herbalist review ten conditions, the first five timed, so the real per-condition rate replaces £150. The site then says "clinically reviewed for safety and accuracy" on those pages, with reviewer name, register and date.
6. Publish the evidence methodology. Two marks: a traditional-use mark and a GRADE-aligned evidence mark. A fixed placebo sentence on every homeopathy pane, never listed first.
7. Rename and narrow the Remedae+ promise. "Interaction notes": static, per remedy, attributed to EMA HMPC and similar, with no reader input of their own medicines. Family profiles and personalised checking wait until after seed and a regulatory view. Hide the Remedae+ signpost until scope and price are signed off, or keep it as a "tell me when" click counted as demand.
8. Keep NHS-derived content free. Tag provenance on every corpus passage; NHS.uk passages carry attribution and a link and never sit behind the paywall.
9. Rebuild the financial model as v2 in September: month 1 is the close month, 105 conditions, a VAT line, 0.3% conversion, three raise cases (£250k, £400k, £500k). Until the close: no hires, no salary, marketing capped at £200 a month, Remedae's Anthropic billing on its own card.
10. Company hygiene before the round: privacy notice, ICO fee, pilot consent, unsubscribe on every email, entity confirmed as a clean separate company, then SEIS and EIS advance assurance and a financial promotion wrapper from a solicitor.

### Decision 17, why the entity question matters

- SEIS and EIS advance assurance is granted to a company, and HMRC looks at what that company owns and does. If the code, the domain, the Supabase project and the Anthropic account sit with Hue & Heal, or with Maria personally, then the investment would be going into a shell with no assets, and assurance is refused or later withdrawn.
- The fix is ordinary: a Remedae limited company (or confirmation it already exists and is clean), an IP assignment from Maria and from Hue & Heal into it for the repository, brand and content, and its own billing for Vercel, Supabase, Anthropic, Resend and the domain. Hue & Heal invoicing Remedae for past work is fine; Hue & Heal owning the work is not.
- It also settles the cap table before any advisor equity (decision 10) or creator terms (decision 11) can be granted, since those are shares in a company that has to exist first.
- Cost and time: an accountant's half day plus a solicitor for the IP assignment; a few hundred pounds; well under a month. It sits ahead of every fundraising step in November, which is why the default is "this month".
- Risk if skipped: an investor's lawyer finds it in due diligence, which delays a close by weeks and reads as carelessness.
