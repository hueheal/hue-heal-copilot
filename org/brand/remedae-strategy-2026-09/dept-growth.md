# Growth department revision, 8 September 2026

## What still holds

- "A library, not a clinic" is the right category and legal posture. Keep it on every page and caption.
- "Your nan was right" is still the best line the brand owns: customer, alternative and transformation in four words.
- The library stays free. The audience is the only proof the pre-seed needs.
- The market evidence is sourced and usable as context, never as a claim about Remedae.
- UK first, diaspora first.

## What is now wrong or stale

- "Launching August at remedae.com" is now "live at remedae.app behind a waitlist gate". Say so everywhere.
- "240+ conditions" is 105 published. "13 traditions, 13 voices" averages six per page. Stop quoting the plan figures externally.
- "Medically reviewed" appears in the deck and financial plan, and no medical advisor is named. Experts note needed before it is used again.
- The financial plan assumes 5,000 readers at launch and £2,000 a month marketing from month one. Neither is a fact: waitlist size is unknown and the pre-seed has not landed.
- Remedae+ is signposted with no price or scope, and collides with the NHS.uk condition that no NHS-derived content sits behind a paywall. Counsel note needed.
- Lines needing an Experts or Counsel note before marketing use: "wrapped in safety guidance", "interaction checking", "interacts with this medication", "when to see a doctor", "nobody experiments on their own body unguided". All read as clinical promises.
- The imagery has moved the brand from "ancient knowledge" to "what you already do at home, checked": one person, one action, a real kitchen. The words must catch up.

## Revised positioning and offer

Positioning, per Dunford:

- Alternatives: NHS.uk or WebMD (no traditions), TikTok (no evidence), a single-tradition app, or asking your mum.
- Unique attributes: traditions side by side on one page, an evidence grade per remedy, plain language, honesty where a tradition is contested.
- Value: one trusted place to check the remedy your family swears by before you use it.
- Who cares most: UK adults raised with home remedies, now caring for a child or parent, searching at 10pm, distrusting both the feed and the leaflet. The smallest viable audience, and the founder's own story.
- Category: free educational health library.

Offer through the value equation:

- Dream outcome: strong once the words above replace "healing knowledge".
- Perceived likelihood: middling. A named advisor and pilot voices would double it.
- Time delay: the gate is a delay we chose. Turn it into weekly invitation waves with a date.
- Effort: the weakest term. Five to ten accordions, disclosure depth three or four, no typeahead. Head of Product owns the fix; Growth needs it before press or paid.

Lead magnet, for the Offer strategist: "Check your family's shelf", one page, five common remedies, what the evidence says, what to ask a pharmacist. Solves one narrow problem completely. Every line goes to the Experts first.

Remedae+: hide the signpost until scope, price logic and the NHS-derived content rule are settled. Finance director signs the price.
