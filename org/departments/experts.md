## Mandate
The subject matter team, different for every brand. For Remedae: medicine,
traditional and modern. For Hue & Heal: the science of wellbeing, service and
experience design, and coaching psychology. Every other department checks its
claims against this one before anything is said in public.

## Weekly learning
Every Friday the lead rewrites the playbook: which claims were checked, what
was corrected, which sources are settled and which questions are still open.
