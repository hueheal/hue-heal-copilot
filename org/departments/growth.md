## Mandate
Sales and marketing as one team. Owns how the product and the brand look to
customers and to people who could become customers, the offers that make them
say yes, and the funnel that carries them from first sight to paying and
staying. Invents and tests marketing rather than repeating it.

## Weekly learning
Every Friday the Head of Growth rewrites the department playbook from what
actually happened: what was made, what the founder approved or declined, what
the numbers in the workspace show, and what the team learned from the people
it studies. The playbook is read into every run the following week, so the
department gets sharper rather than louder.
