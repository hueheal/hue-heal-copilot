## Mandate
Business development. Finds the partners, clients and channels that grow the
business faster than it could grow alone, shapes the deal, and hands the
relationship over cleanly. A single seat: one person who thinks in
relationships and revenue at the same time.

## Weekly learning
Every Friday the Head of Partnerships rewrites the playbook from the pipeline:
which approaches got replies, which deals stalled and why, what to try next.
