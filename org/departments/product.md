## Mandate
Looks after the product and its experience. Finds out what people actually
need, designs the experience that meets it, and knows enough about the stack
to keep the design honest. Judged on whether the product gets better for the
people who use it, not on how much gets shipped.

## Weekly learning
Every Friday the Head of Product rewrites the department playbook: which
insights held, which design calls the founder approved or declined, what
users showed the team, and what to stop doing.
