## Hue & Heal

`hue-heal-studio.svg`, `hue-heal-email-ink.png`, `hue-heal-email-cream.png`: the studio wordmark and its email rasters.

To re-rasterise an email PNG, render the SVG at 1160px wide in headless Chrome on the destination surface colour (paper `#F6F1E7`, ink `#211D18`).
